# Dirty Birds Advanced Metrics and Waiver Evidence Contract

## Status
- Hourly Sleeper snapshot + trade scouting, retrospective valuation and waiver ownership scanner: implemented.
- External snap/route/target/workload datasets, verified player health history, dated rest-of-season projections: **not yet connected**.
- Player-level recommendations: **not authorized** until verified sources and coverage are supplied.

## Approved inputs
`data/ros_projections.json` (not autogenerated):
```json
{"as_of":"YYYY-MM-DD","scoring":"half_ppr","source":"provider and URL","players":{"Player Name":{"ppg":12.5,"status":"active","games_remaining":8}}}
```

`data/external_metrics.json` (optional, sourced):
```json
{"as_of":"YYYY-MM-DD","source":"provider and URL","players":{"Player Name":{"source":"provider and URL","as_of":"YYYY-MM-DD","snap_share_pct":65,"snap_count":50,"routes_run":30,"route_participation_pct":80,"targets_per_game":6.5,"target_share_pct":20,"air_yards_share_pct":23,"rush_attempts_per_game":0,"red_zone_opportunities_per_game":1,"yards_per_route_run":2.1,"yards_after_contact_per_attempt":null,"games_played":6,"games_missed":1,"injury_designation":"official report date + designation","practice_participation":"official report date + level","team_offense_context":"evidence","bye_week":9,"remaining_schedule_strength":"method+source","playoff_schedule_strength":"method+source"}}}
```

Percent variables are 0-100, not 0-1. `null` means missing, not zero. Actual routes, target share, red-zone usage, QB changes, touch distribution, game script, injury designations, durability history, weather and usage variability need dated sourcing.

`trade_simulator.py` exposes coverage of advanced fields for all players in a proposed deal. It does **not** silently adjust projection PPG or claim an injury prediction when advanced evidence is missing.

## Waiver criteria
- Confirm available in Sleeper league `data/latest.json`; roster ownership is a snapshot, not a claim guarantee.
- Confirm active, pending/waiver state, status, role and eligibility.
- Prioritize volume/snap trends, opportunities/targets, efficiency, offensive environment, short-term schedule, playoff usability, injury information, FAAB cost, future role and our actual positional need.
- Match to replaceable Dirty Birds bench player; compare projected net starter and FLEX gain, not only raw player scores.
- **Never** substitute missing data with zero or automatic top-pick rank.
- Injury history is probabilistic context, not a reliable medical forecast of future injury.

## Execution
The hourly `sleeper-sync.yml` workflow runs `update.py`, `radar.py`, `scouting.py`, `valuation.py` and `waiver.py`; new waiver files are written as `data/WAIVER_WATCHLIST.md` and `data/waiver_watchlist.json` after a successful run.

Manual `trade-simulator.yml` runs synthetic unit tests and optional trade comparisons, provided ALL corresponding players have sourced projected PPG. It never executes Sleeper transactions.

No trades or waiver claims are submitted without Coach's direct action/approval.
