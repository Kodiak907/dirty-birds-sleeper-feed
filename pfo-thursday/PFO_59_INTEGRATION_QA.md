## Additional large-evidence audit
- Verified against official Cloudflare Workers Web Crypto, Streams and R2 API documentation (October 2026): workers expose `crypto.DigestStream('SHA-256')` and `FixedLengthStream(length)`, and `R2.put(key, readable)` accepts an appropriately sized stream.
- Files above 8MiB are verified by their first bytes, streamed through concurrent R2 and SHA-256 consumers and recorded in D1 only after both finish; failed uploads attempt to delete any incomplete object. Small images, PDFs and videos retain buffered magic-number verification.
- Streaming has been exercised using fake Cloudflare streams, DB and bucket. Production Cloudflare and Apple Safari end-to-end behavior is **not** verified. Do not claim deployment passed.
